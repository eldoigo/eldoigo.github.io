ANCO© PN-LOC© Nut

Bon Accord Engineering

Are the Western EU stockist of the ANCO® PN-LOC® Nut, manufactured only in Texas.

The ANCO® PN-LOC® is a proprietary, all metal, self-locking nut that features a stainless-steel ratchet pin.

Coupled with a unique controlled lock indention, this non-breakable ratchet pin limits damage to bolt threads, and the removal of galvanizing while ensuring consistent locking torque.
